## 🎯 What are Binaural Beats?

When you play 100 Hz in one ear and 110 Hz in the other, your brain perceives a 10 Hz "beat" - and begins to resonate at that frequency (Alpha state). This is called the Frequency Following Response.

Different frequencies induce different mental states:
- **Delta (0.5-4 Hz):** Deep sleep, healing
- **Theta (4-7 Hz):** Deep meditation, creativity  
- **Alpha (8-12 Hz):** Relaxed awareness, visualization
- **Beta (12-30 Hz):** Active thinking, focus
- **Gamma (30-100 Hz):** Peak awareness
